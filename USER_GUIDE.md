## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection
- Valid Ghana phone number for verification
- Ghana Card ID for registration

### First-Time Setup

1. **Registration**
   - Visit the registration page
   - Select your role (Customer, ISP, or Staff)
   - Fill in your details including Ghana Card ID
   - Verify your phone number
   - Set up your password

2. **Profile Setup**
   - Complete your profile information
   - Add your address with GhanaPost GPS code
   - Upload a profile picture (optional)
   - Set notification preferences

3. **Dashboard Access**
   - Log in with your credentials
   - Access your role-specific dashboard
   - Explore available features

## Customer Guide

### Creating a Job Request

1. Navigate to "Create Job Request" from the dashboard
2. Select the service category:
   - Electrical
   - Plumbing
   - Carpentry
   - Painting
   - Cleaning
   - Air Conditioning
   - Masonry
   - Roofing
   - Aluminium
   - General Repairs
   - Generator
   - Solar Installation
   - Security
3. Provide a detailed description of the work needed
4. Set priority level:
   - Low (non-urgent)
   - Normal (standard)
   - High (important)
   - Urgent (immediate attention)
5. Enter your address (you can use GhanaPost GPS code)
6. Choose preferred date and time
7. Add any additional notes
8. Submit your request

### Viewing Job Status

1. Go to "My Jobs" from the dashboard
2. Click on any job to view details
3. Status updates include:
   - **New**: Job created, awaiting assignment
   - **Assigned**: ISP assigned, awaiting completion
   - **In Progress**: ISP is working on the job
   - **Completed**: Job finished, awaiting rating
   - **Cancelled**: Job was cancelled

### Understanding Quotations

1. When a quotation is created, you'll receive a notification
2. Review the quotation details:
   - Labour cost
   - Materials cost
   - Travel cost
   - Total amount
   - AI confidence score
3. Accept or reject the quotation
4. If rejected, you can request a revision
5. Accepted quotations proceed to job assignment

### Rating ISPs

After job completion:

1. You'll receive a rating request
2. Rate the ISP on four criteria (1-5 scale):
   - **Quality**: Quality of work performed
   - **Timeliness**: Punctuality and adherence to schedule
   - **Professionalism**: Professional conduct and behavior
   - **Communication**: Responsiveness and clarity
3. Add a written review (optional)
4. Submit your rating
5. Note: Ratings can be edited within 24 hours

### Mobile App Features

- Create jobs on the go
- Track job status in real-time
- Receive push notifications
- Accept quotations quickly
- Rate ISPs after completion

## Ghana-Specific Features

### Ghana Card ID Verification

- Your Ghana Card ID is required for registration
- Format: `GHA-123456789-0`
- Used for identity verification
- Required for certain service types

### GhanaPost GPS

- Use your GhanaPost GPS code for accurate location
- Format: `AK-039-5021`
- Helps ISPs find your location quickly
- Improves matching accuracy

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: Cannot log in**
- Solution: Check your email and password
- Reset password if needed
- Ensure your account is active

**Problem: Job not showing**
- Solution: Check job status
- Contact support if status is incorrect
- Verify job was submitted successfully

**Problem: Quotation not received**
- Solution: Check your notifications
- Ensure job is assigned to an ISP
- Contact staff for assistance

**Problem: ISP not responding**
- Solution: Check ISP availability status
- Contact staff to reassign
- Report the issue

**Problem: Rating not submitting**
- Solution: Check your internet connection
- Ensure job is marked as completed

## FAQ

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Go to Settings > Security > Change Password

**Q: Can I cancel a job after it's assigned?**
A: Yes, but contact staff first to arrange with the ISP

**Q: How are quotations calculated?**
A: Quotations use AI analysis based on job complexity, location, and ISP availability

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the service?**
A: Rate the ISP honestly and contact staff for resolution

**Q: How do I become an ISP?**
A: Register with ISP role and complete your profile with certifications

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures

**Q: Can I use the system outside Ghana?**
